parsed.gg ↗

// overview

Every CS2 match you play, broken down automatically. parsed.gg pulls your matchmaking demos straight from Steam and turns them into something you can actually improve from — round-by-round replays, career stats that mean something, and coaching that talks tactics instead of just numbers.

Most stats sites tell you what happened. parsed.gg tells you why — and what to do about it. It learns the strategic shape of each round, shows you the moments that decided it, and points you at how stronger players handle the same situation. Your matches, your server, your data.

// how it works

ingest
Connect Steam once. Every matchmaking game pulls in and gets analyzed automatically — or drop in a demo file to break down on the spot.
replay
Re-watch any round on a clean 2D tactical map. Follow any player, see exactly what they could (and couldn't) see, and scrub the action like a video.
stats
A career dashboard that means something: K/D, ADR, win rate, recent form, and where you actually win or lose on each map.
coaching
AI that learns each round's shape, not just the scoreboard — what the play was going for, where it fell apart, and how stronger players handle the same spot.
private
Your demos stay yours. Private by design, nothing shared to third parties.
